Privacy policy

Lift Board for iPhone. Updated November 17, 2026.

Lift Board is free. There are no ads and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. That includes your goal, your activities, your workouts and sets, your weight and body fat, cycle notes and how you feel. Your plan is worked out on the phone too. Lift Board doesn't send any of it to us or to anyone else.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report so we can fix the bug. A report holds the error, where in the app it happened, the app version, the iOS version and the iPhone model. It never holds your workouts, weights, body stats, notes, name or email, and it isn't linked to you. Crash reports are handled by Sentry, our crash reporting service.

Things you choose to share

  • When you export your data or share a workout card, the file goes only where you send it.
  • Reminders and rest alerts are set up on your phone. They don't go through a server.
  • The workout on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island comes from the app on your phone.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Your data, then Delete account removes everything Lift Board has stored on your phone. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Lift Board isn't meant for children under 13.

Health

Lift Board gives general training and nutrition estimates, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before big changes, especially if you're pregnant, injured or managing a health condition.
